## History (what the Our Story page is built from)

- **Before 2007**: Rose moved to the U.S. for work as a nurse's aide and sent money home to Nilo and Rocel for years until they could join her. *(New York Times, Feb 2015, via When In Manila's summary)*
- **2007**: First store, **RJ Sariling Atin Fast Food & Oriental Grocery**, 1561 Lakewood Rd, Toms River, NJ. "RJ" = Rocel + Julia; "Rocel" = **Ro**sario + **Cel**so. They chose food because it's a basic necessity. *(Palawan Daily News column, Jan 2020; Roadtrippers listing)*
- **2013–14**: Nilo prayed for a New York location; a relative in Elmhurst told him about a vacant former furniture showroom on Queens Blvd near Queens Center. About six months of permits; the landlord helped financially. *(Palawan Daily News)*
- **April 5, 2014**: Elmhurst opens, the first Filipino grocery on the south side of Queens Blvd. "A little over seven hundred" customers on opening day. Rocel (24) ran the store. Early best-sellers: barbecue chicken and pork, turon. *(DNAinfo, Apr 2014; Palawan Daily News)*
- **2014**: CBS New York names it one of NYC's 5 best restaurants for Filipino food (within ~5 months of opening). *(CBS New York, Jessica Allen; Palawan Daily News)*
- **Feb 2015**: New York Times "Hungry City" review; The Village Voice's eleven best breakfasts in NYC (2015).
- **Sept 3, 2016**: Sariling Atin Woodside Grill & Pastry Shop opens, 40-09 69th St, Woodside. *(their Facebook flyer)*
- **Sept 2018**: Bergenfield, NJ store. **Nov 23, 2019**: Jersey City store (125 Mallory Ave). **2020**: lease signed for 404 Tonnele Ave, Jersey City. *(Palawan Daily News; Jersey Digs, Sept 2020)*
- **Jan 2022**: The Infatuation, 8.3 ("Great"); listed in its Best Filipino Restaurants in NYC guide.
- **May 31, 2023**: Partnership with **ATLAS Express Padala** for balikbayan box drop-off and pick-up (pick-up $55, drop-off $45 at the time). *(Facebook)*
- **Jan 20, 2024**: New Woodside branch, **68-10 Roosevelt Ave** (near Jollibee and Red Ribbon): groceries, balikbayan boxes, remittance. *(Facebook)*
- **April 5, 2024**: Ten years in Elmhurst. Their post: *Sariling Atin, after all, is "our very own" place to celebrate our Filipino culture.* *(Facebook)*
- **Sept 17, 2024**: Turo-turo restaurant moved back inside the grocery store; turo-turo 8 AM – 9 PM, grocery 9 AM – 8 PM at the time. *(Facebook)*
- **2025**: ATLAS flyer: "Sariling Atin, Inc., ang nag-iisang partner/agent sa Northeast America region." Lists Queens, Toms River (732) 244-4493 and Bergenfield (the Queens and Bergenfield numbers are swapped on the flyer). *(Facebook)*

## Food

- **Catering menu (their own, posted on Facebook ~2025)**: full/half tray prices for 70+ dishes, tray size 12.5 × 10.5 × 2.5 in, one full tray feeds 10–15, "prices are subject to change without prior notice," lechon at market price (medium 30–35 lbs, large 36–40 lbs). This is the source for every price on `/catering` (`src/data/menu.ts`).
- **Party tray menu (same source)**: 12 trays for $449 + tax: 1 beef, 1 chicken, 1 pork, 1 seafood, 1 vegetable, 1 appetizer, 2 noodles, 2 desserts, 2 rice, from a fixed list, "NO changes, NO exceptions."
- **Yelp's menu page** shows an older version of the same menu (e.g. $45–$65 trays, party set $375). The site uses the newer Facebook version.
- **Counter**: combo meals of 1, 2 or 3 ulam with rice or noodles; take-out by the container (S/M/L); prices on the chalkboard ("Sariling Atin, 'our own'"). Extras on the board: chicharon bulaklak, chicken chicharon, crispy pata, BBQ sticks, lechon kawali, lechon belly.
- **Most mentioned in reviews and press**: sisig, pork BBQ, pork adobo, Bicol express, dinuguan, kare-kare, lechon kawali, crispy pata, kalderetang kambing, pancit malabon, vegetable lumpia, turon, halo-halo, rolled lechon belly with lemongrass.
- **Grocery items named in press**: Datu Puti cane vinegar, Andok's lechon sauce, egg cracklets, adobo-flavored corn nuts (NYT); Ding Dong, Squidee (Infatuation). Frozen ube, bangus, tinapa, chicken tocino (Woodside listing).
- **Payment**: Visa, Mastercard, Discover, American Express stickers at the register (their Facebook photo). One listing mentions a $10 card minimum (unverified, not on the site).

## Confirm with the client before launch

1. **Domain.** The site is built for `https://www.sarilingatinelmhurst.com` (their old, lapsed domain) as a placeholder. Change `SITE_URL` in `src/data/site.ts` once the real one is bought.
2. **Hours.** Google/Yelp say 9–8 daily; their catering menu says 9–9; the Sept 2024 post said turo-turo 8–9 and grocery 9–8. Which is right today?
3. **Prices.** Are the 2025 catering prices and the $449 party set still current? Prices for dessert trays (the site says "ask us")?
4. **Seating.** Is there still a dining area since the counter moved back into the grocery?
5. **Other stores.** Are Woodside (68-10 Roosevelt Ave), Bergenfield (54 E Main St) and Toms River (1561 Lakewood Rd) all open and family-run? Jersey City isn't on the 2025 flyer, so the site leaves it off. Edit `SISTER_STORES` in `src/data/story.ts`.
6. **Padala.** Which remittance company/companies does the Elmhurst counter use today (the 2015 sign showed Ria; the Woodside sign shows LOGO)? The site keeps it generic. ATLAS pick-up/drop-off rates?
7. **The family story.** OK to share Rose's nurse's-aide/padala story and the daughters' names (Rocel, Julia)? Both were in the New York Times and the Palawan column, but it's their call.
8. **Email / socials.** No email, Instagram or TikTok found. If they have one, add it to `src/data/site.ts` (and consider a contact form).
9. **Halo-halo.** Seasonal? The menu says "ask if it's on today."
10. **Photos.** Fresh photos of the Elmhurst storefront and of sisig, kare-kare, Bicol express and dinuguan would replace the painted name tiles (see README → Photos).
